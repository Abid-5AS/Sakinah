## Vision

To create a digital sanctuary that helps Muslims worldwide, particularly in Bangladesh, develop a profound and personal relationship with Allah through the internalization of His Beautiful Names (Asma ul Husna), transforming daily experiences into opportunities for spiritual growth and reflection.

## Mission

Build a privacy-first, offline-capable mobile application that serves as a personal spiritual companion, guiding users from basic memorization to deep internalization of Islamic teachings, with the ultimate goal of making the reflective practice so natural that the app itself becomes unnecessary.

## The Problem We're Solving

### 1. Disconnection from Divine Attributes
Many Muslims memorize the 99 Names of Allah but struggle to:
- Connect these names to their daily experiences
- Understand the deeper meanings beyond translation
- Apply these attributes in their personal spiritual journey
- See Allah's work in their everyday lives

### 2. Privacy Concerns in Digital Spirituality
Existing spiritual apps often:
- Require internet connectivity
- Store personal reflections on external servers
- Lack transparency about data usage
- Don't provide adequate security for private thoughts

### 3. Authenticity and Trust Issues
- Many Islamic apps lack proper citation and authentication of sources
- Users cannot verify the authenticity of Hadith
- Mix of authentic and weak narrations without distinction
- No access to original context or scholarly commentary

### 4. Cultural Disconnect
- Most apps are designed with Middle Eastern or Western perspectives
- Lack of consideration for South Asian (particularly Bangladeshi) Islamic traditions
- Missing local language support (Bengali)
- No adaptation to local cultural practices and needs

## The Solution

**Sakinah** is a comprehensive spiritual ecosystem consisting of four interconnected pillars:

### Pillar 1: The Sanctuary (Private Journal)
A 100% private, encrypted journaling space where users can:
- Record daily reflections, thoughts, and duas
- Express themselves freely without fear of exposure
- Build a personal spiritual timeline
- Create a safe space for honest self-reflection

**Key Innovation**: Zero-knowledge architecture - not even the app developers can access user journals.

### Pillar 2: The Library (Knowledge Vault)
A complete, offline, authenticated Islamic reference library containing:
- Full Quran with multiple translations (including Bengali)
- Major Hadith collections with authenticity grading
- 99 Names of Allah with etymological analysis
- Selected Tafsir from renowned scholars
- All pre-loaded and verifiable

**Key Innovation**: Trust through transparency - every source is cited, authenticated, and locally stored.

### Pillar 3: The Companion (Reflective Engine)
An intelligent, on-device analysis system that:
- Reads journal entries privately on the device
- Identifies themes, emotions, and spiritual opportunities
- Suggests relevant Names, verses, and Hadith
- Provides contextual spiritual guidance
- Never sends data to external servers

**Key Innovation**: On-device AI that respects privacy while providing intelligent insights.

### Pillar 4: The Guide (Learning Path)
A structured system for spiritual growth featuring:
- Spaced repetition for memorizing the 99 Names
- Interactive flashcards with pronunciation
- Visual connection maps between related Names
- Progress tracking toward "graduation"
- Customizable learning pace

**Key Innovation**: Gamification without game-ification - serious learning with helpful structure.

## Core Philosophy

### 1. The Graduation Goal
Unlike typical apps that aim for maximum engagement and retention, Sakinah's success is measured by user **graduation**:
- The user has internalized the practice of spiritual reflection
- They naturally see Allah's attributes in daily life
- They no longer need the app as a crutch
- The app has fulfilled its purpose as a teacher

This is inspired by the Islamic principle: **"The best teacher is one who makes students independent."**

### 2. Privacy as an Islamic Value
Islam highly values privacy (Sitr). The Prophet (ﷺ) said: *"All of my ummah will be forgiven except those who sin openly."* (Bukhari)

Sakinah embodies this by:
- Keeping all personal reflections encrypted and local
- Never transmitting private data
- Providing full user control over their data
- Building trust through technical transparency

### 3. Authenticity Above All
The Prophet (ﷺ) warned: *"Whoever narrates a hadith from me which he knows is a lie, then he is one of the liars."* (Muslim)

We ensure authenticity by:
- Including only Sahih and Hasan Hadith (with clear grading)
- Citing scholarly sources for all interpretations
- Allowing users to verify everything in the built-in library
- Regular content review by qualified scholars

### 4. Ikhlas (Sincerity) in Design
The app is designed with sincerity, not for profit maximization:
- No ads
- No data harvesting
- No manipulative engagement tactics
- No subscription traps
- Free core features (with optional support for continued development)

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience
**Bangladeshi Muslims (18-45 years old)**
- University students and young professionals
- Comfortable with technology
- Seeking deeper spiritual connection
- Value privacy and authenticity
- Bilingual (Bengali/English)

### Secondary Audience
**Global Muslim Community**
- Anyone seeking to deepen their understanding of Allah's Names
- Muslims in diaspora maintaining spiritual connection
- Converts to Islam learning foundational concepts
- Parents teaching children about Islamic spirituality

### User Personas

#### Persona 1: "Thoughtful Tahmid"
- 24-year-old university student in Dhaka
- Studies Engineering
- Prays regularly but feels spiritually disconnected
- Wants to understand Islam beyond rituals
- Concerned about online privacy
- Tech-savvy, uses multiple apps

#### Persona 2: "Reflective Raisa"
- 31-year-old working professional
- Works in IT sector
- Strong faith but limited time for deep study
- Keeps a personal diary
- Wants to connect daily experiences with faith
- Values authentic sources

#### Persona 3: "Learning Labib"
- 19-year-old student preparing for HSC
- Recently became more practicing
- Wants to memorize 99 Names
- Needs structured learning approach
- Easily distracted by social media
- Prefers Bengali language content

## Success Metrics

### User Success (Primary Metrics)
1. **Internalization Rate**: Users who report recognizing Allah's Names in daily life without app prompts
2. **Graduation Rate**: Users who reduce app usage because they've internalized the practice
3. **Consistency**: Users who maintain regular journaling for 90+ days
4. **Learning Completion**: Users who memorize all 99 Names

### Product Success (Secondary Metrics)
1. **Privacy Confidence**: User trust scores regarding data privacy
2. **Content Trust**: User confidence in authenticity of Islamic content
3. **Offline Usage**: Percentage of app usage without internet
4. **Bengali Usage**: Adoption rate of Bengali interface and content

### Impact Metrics (Long-term)
1. **Community Growth**: Organic referrals and testimonials
2. **Scholarly Recognition**: Endorsements from Islamic scholars
3. **Educational Adoption**: Use in Islamic schools and madrasahs
4. **Social Impact**: User-reported improvements in spiritual well-being

## Unique Value Proposition

#### 1. Goal-Oriented Design
- **Other apps**: Maximize engagement and screen time
- **Sakinah**: Maximize learning and enable graduation

#### 2. Privacy Architecture
- **Other apps**: Cloud-based, data collection, analytics
- **Sakinah**: On-device, encrypted, zero-knowledge

#### 3. Content Authenticity
- **Other apps**: Mixed sources, unclear authentication
- **Sakinah**: Verified sources, transparent grading, full citations

#### 4. Intelligent Reflection
- **Other apps**: Generic daily reminders and quotes
- **Sakinah**: Personalized, context-aware spiritual guidance

#### 5. Cultural Sensitivity
- **Other apps**: One-size-fits-all approach
- **Sakinah**: Bangladeshi-aware, Bengali support, culturally appropriate

#### 6. Complete Offline Functionality
- **Other apps**: Require internet for key features

#### 7. Educational Foundation
- **Other apps**: Passive consumption of content

## Islamic Scholarly Foundation

### Key Islamic Concepts Implemented

1. **Tafakkur (Contemplation)**
   - Encouraged in Quran: "Do they not reflect upon themselves?" (30:8)
   - The journal feature facilitates daily tafakkur

2. **Dhikr (Remembrance)**
   - "Remember Me, I will remember you" (2:152)
   - The 99 Names system is structured dhikr

3. **Muraqaba (Mindfulness of Allah)**
   - "He is with you wherever you are" (57:4)
   - The reflective engine trains users in muraqaba

4. **Tazkiyah (Purification of the Soul)**
   - "Indeed he succeeds who purifies it" (91:9)
   - The entire app journey is a tazkiyah process

5. **Taleem (Teaching with Wisdom)**
   - "Invite to the way of your Lord with wisdom" (16:125)
   - Gradual, respectful teaching methodology

## Ethical Commitments

1. **No Monetization of Faith**
   - Core spiritual features always free
   - Optional donation-based support model
   - No ads or data selling

2. **Accessibility First**

3. **Open Development**
